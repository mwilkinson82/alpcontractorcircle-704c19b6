## Email 3 — Causation lesson

**Subject:** A late activity is not automatically a project delay.

Something happened late.

That does not yet prove the project was delayed.

The question is what was critical when the event occurred, what path controlled completion, what the schedule record can support and which analysis method fits the facts you actually have.

That is why the intensive does not teach one canned schedule method.

We work through:

- Baseline integrity.
- Updates and data dates.
- Critical path and longest path.
- Time Impact Analysis.
- Windows analysis.
- As-planned versus as-built.
- Collapsed as-built.
- Concurrency, pacing, mitigation and acceleration.

Then every attendee completes a Delay Method Selection Worksheet built around the record—not preference.

**Prove the delay:** https://alpcontractorcircle.com/delay-intensive
